Privacy Policy – Snow Dodger

Effective date: June 12, 2026
Developer: DodgeLab (kongzhebing@gmail.com)

1. Information We Collect

Snow Dodger does not require account creation or login. The app collects the following data solely to display advertisements and process in-app purchases:

  • Advertising ID (Google Ad ID) — collected and shared with Google AdMob to serve personalized or non-personalized ads.
  • Purchase tokens — processed through Google Play Billing to fulfill in-app purchases. No payment information is stored by the developer.

2. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — displays ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing — processes in-app purchases.
  • Firebase Realtime Database — stores anonymous leaderboard data (nickname, score, level). No personally identifiable information is stored.

3. Data Sharing

Advertising ID is shared with Google AdMob for ad personalization. No other personal data is shared with third parties.

4. Data Retention

Game progress is stored locally on the device via WebView localStorage. Leaderboard data is retained on Firebase until the monthly season resets.

5. Children's Privacy

Snow Dodger is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Your Choices

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device's Google Settings. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted at this URL.
