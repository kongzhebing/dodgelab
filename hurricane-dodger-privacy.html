The app has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, photos, contacts or precise location. 1. Data stored only on your device Game progress, coins, settings, unlocked aircraft, items and your selected language are saved in the app's local storage on your device. This data is not sent to us and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. 2. Data collected and shared Data Purpose Processed by Advertising ID, device model, OS version, approximate region (from IP address) Showing banner, interstitial and rewarded ads; ad measurement and fraud prevention Google AdMob Gameplay events (for example level start/end, tutorial steps, ad requests, purchases completed), app instance ID, device model, OS version, app version, approximate region Understanding how the game is played and improving it; app stability Google Firebase (Analytics) A randomly generated 8-character player code and your monthly best score Global monthly leaderboard Google Firebase (Realtime Database) Purchase status (whether a product was bought) Delivering in-app purchases Google Play Billing The player code is random and is not linked to your name or any account. We do not sell personal data. Payment details are handled entirely by Google Play; we never receive them. 3. Public storm data To show the "Daily Storm" mission, the app requests publicly available storm information from the U.S. National Hurricane Center website (nhc.noaa.gov). No personal data is included in this request; as with any web request, your IP address is visible to that server. 4. Notifications If you allow notifications, reminders (such as the daily recon order) are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data is sent to a server. You can turn them off in the in-game pause menu or in your device settings. 5. Advertising choices You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads / Privacy > Ads). If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time with the "Ad privacy choices" link at the bottom of the home screen. 6. Third-party policies Google AdMob and Firebase: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services 6-1. International transfer Leaderboard and analytics data are processed on Google infrastructure, including servers in the Asia–Southeast region (Singapore). 7. Children The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. 8. Security and retention All data sent from the app uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Leaderboard entries are stored per month. Analytics and advertising data are kept according to Google's retention settings. 9. Your rights and deletion requests To ask for your leaderboard entry to be deleted, or for any privacy question, email us. We will respond within 30 days. 10. Changes We will update this page if our practices change and revise the date above.
