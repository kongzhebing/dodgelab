# Privacy Policy — Traffic Dodger

**App name:** Traffic Dodger  
**Developer:** DodgeLab  
**Last updated:** June 2026  

## 1. Information We Collect

**Automatically collected:**

- App usage statistics (via Google Play)

- Settings and preferences
- Purchase history (via Google Play Billing)

Traffic Dodger is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

All game data is stored locally on your device. We do not transmit personal data to our servers.
