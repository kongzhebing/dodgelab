Privacy Policy — Deep Dodger

App: Deep Dodger (com.dodgelab.deepdodger) · Developer: Southport
Effective date: June 24, 2026 · Last updated: June 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Southport (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use the mobile game Deep Dodger (the “App”). By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

a. Information stored only on your device

Your game progress, best score, in-game currency balance, and settings (such as language and sound) are saved locally on your device. This information is not transmitted to us and is removed when you uninstall the App.

b. Information you choose to provide

If you submit a score to the global ranking, the App sends a nickname you choose together with your in-game result (e.g., dive depth) and the relevant month to our ranking database. Please do not use your real name or any sensitive personal information as your nickname.

c. Information collected automatically

  • Usage and analytics data — via Google Firebase Analytics, we collect app events (e.g., game start, game over, ad views, purchases), approximate engagement, app instance identifiers, and basic device/OS information to understand how the App is used and to improve it.
  • Advertising data — via Google AdMob, an advertising identifier and device information may be collected to serve and measure ads.

d. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your payment card details. We may receive a confirmation that a purchase was completed in order to deliver the purchased item.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate core features such as global rankings.
  • To display and measure advertising.
  • To understand usage, fix bugs, and improve the App.
  • To process and restore in-app purchases.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following Google services, which may collect and process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase — Analytics and Realtime Database (rankings) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play Billing (purchases) — policies.google.com/privacy

4. Advertising

Ads are served by Google AdMob and may be personalized based on your advertising identifier. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads). Where required, we request consent for personalized ads.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the third-party service providers listed above as necessary to operate the App.

6. International Transfer

Our ranking and analytics data is processed on Google’s infrastructure, including servers in the Asia–Southeast region. By using the App you consent to this processing.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Data Retention

Locally stored data remains until you clear it or uninstall the App. Ranking entries are retained per month for leaderboard purposes. Analytics and advertising data are retained according to Google’s policies.

9. Your Choices

  • Reset or limit your advertising ID in device settings.
  • Uninstall the App to remove locally stored data.
  • Contact us to request deletion of a ranking nickname/entry.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above reflects the latest version.
